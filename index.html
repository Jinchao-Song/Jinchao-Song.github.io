Geospatial science · Urban sustainability

Sensing, modeling, and understanding urban systems

I use GIS, remote sensing, spatial modeling and geospatial big data to investigate how urban form and human activities shape resource use and environmental conditions. My work connects building material stocks, urban functional zones, population distribution, urban heat and traffic emissions.

Featured research · Nature Cities · 2026

Weighing 600 million buildings reveals global urban material inequality and efficiency paths

Our global, building-level assessment covers 606 million structures and connects material stocks with urban form, inequality and future material demand.
